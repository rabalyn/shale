# Weekend trip

We leave on Saturday morning and come back on Sunday afternoon. The cabin is by the lake, about two hours north of the city.

## Route

Take the ring road north, leave at exit 14 and follow the signs to the lake. The last part is a gravel road, so drive slowly.

## Food

We cook on the fire. Everyone brings one dish for the first evening, and there is a shop in the village for the rest.

## Weather

The forecast is still open. Check it again on Thursday evening before packing.

## Packing list

- tent
- sleeping bags
- boat
- food

## Contact

Call Mia if the plans change.
