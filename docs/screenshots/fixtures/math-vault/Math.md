# Formulas

A formula between dollar signs sits in the text, like $E = mc^2$ or the roots $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ of a quadratic. A price such as $5 and $10 is no formula.

Two dollar signs on lines of their own set a formula in display style, centred:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^2 = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} \qquad \int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
$$

Matrices, cases and delimiters that grow with what they hold:

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}, \qquad |x| = \begin{cases} x & x \ge 0 \\ -x & x < 0 \end{cases}, \qquad \left( \frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{x}} \right)
$$

| Name | Formula |
|:---|:---:|
| Circle | $A = \pi r^2$ |
| Euler | $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ |
