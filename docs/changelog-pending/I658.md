### Added

- The export dialog of a note now has a **Fonts** row: when the note (`print-font`, `print-font-sans`, `print-font-mono`) or the vault's settings (`print_font`, `print_font_sans`, `print_font_mono` in `.shale/settings.json`) name a font, it says which one the export would use and where it was found (the vault's `fonts/` folder or an installed font), and warns when a font is missing or its licence does not allow embedding it. The fonts are looked up in the background (#658). The built-in PDF export uses them (below); the LaTeX export follows.
- The **built-in PDF export uses the fonts** of `print-font`, `print-font-sans` (headings, title, contents and page numbers, else the text font) and `print-font-mono` (code), or the vault's settings, with the family's **real bold and italic faces** instead of the imitation. A face a family lacks is imitated and the export says so once; a font that is missing, cannot be read or whose licence does not allow embedding it is a warning and the next choice is used, never a failed export. A font is embedded cut down to the characters the PDF uses (whole when its licence says so), and a font with PostScript outlines (`.otf`) is drawn as outlines. When a font may be an installed one, the export waits for the font lookup (at most 20 seconds). The warnings are shown after the export (#658).
- A **folder's PDF has one font**, the top note's: a chapter's own `print-font*` is ignored with a warning, and the title page, chapter titles, running headers and contents are set in the book's fonts (#658).
