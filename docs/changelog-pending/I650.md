### Added

- **Citations and cross-references in the Word export.** A note with the thesis syntax (`bibliography:`, `[@key]`, `{#fig:x}`, `@fig:x`) now comes out right through **Export to Word…**: pandoc's `--citeproc` formats the citations with the note's `.bib` file and the same style the PDF uses (`citation-style:`, or a `.csl` file of the vault) and prints the list of references at the end or at `<!-- shale: bibliography -->`; labelled figures, tables and equations get their number and a caption in Word's caption styles (above or below as `figure-caption:` and `table-caption:` say), and the references are written as "Abbildung 3" or "Figure 3" by the note's `language:`. Keys and labels that do not resolve are named in the toast and stay as written. No extra program is needed: Shale numbers and resolves the references itself, so the numbers are the ones of the other exports (ADR-0041, Closes #650).
