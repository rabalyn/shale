### Added

- Import: a follow-up mail continues its thread. The Import dialog finds notes of the same conversation by the reply headers of the mail (`In-Reply-To` and `References`, never the subject), offers "Continues [[note]]" with a tick box, puts the new note in the folder of that note, and links the notes with `previous` and `next` properties in date order (a `thread-id` ties them together; only the new note and at most two neighbours change, and only their `previous` / `next` lines). An older mail asks "Keep the thread in date order?" (Update or Leave the chain). The new note and the neighbour lines are one entry of the file history. Posts follow with the feeds (#575).
- A new **Thread** view in the right sidebar lists the mails of the open note's thread, oldest first. Ctrl+Shift+PageUp and Ctrl+Shift+PageDown step to the previous and next mail of the thread, Ctrl+Shift+T opens the view and gives it the keyboard (#575).
