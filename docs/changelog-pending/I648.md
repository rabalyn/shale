### Added

- **Thesis writing in the built-in PDF and the site export** (no TeX needed): citations are written as the chosen `citation-style:` formats them and link to their entry, figures, tables and equations with a label (`{#fig:x}`, `{#tbl:x}`, `{#eq:x}`) get their number and caption ("Abbildung 3: …", tables above and figures below unless `table-caption:` and `figure-caption:` say otherwise, equation numbers at the right margin), references (`@fig:x`) become "Figure 3" as a link to the place, and the bibliography stands at the end or at `<!-- shale: bibliography -->`. `<!-- shale: list-of-figures -->` and `<!-- shale: list-of-tables -->` print the lists of figures and tables. A key or label that does not resolve is a warning of the export. A thesis of several notes in one PDF follows in a later phase (#648).
