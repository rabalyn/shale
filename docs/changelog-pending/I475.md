### Added

- Conflict copies of Syncthing, Dropbox, Nextcloud, ownCloud and Seafile (`Note.sync-conflict-….md`, `Note (… conflicted copy …).md`, `Note_conflict-….md`, `Note (SFConflict …).md`) and Shale's own `Note (conflict …).md` are recognised. They carry a "conflict" badge in the file list, and the note shows a banner "Another device changed this note at the same time" with Compare (the changes, or the Markdown side by side), "Keep this one" (its text goes into the original) and "Keep the other"; the copy goes to the trash, never deleted for good. While the original exists, the copy is left out of search, links, the graph, backlinks, tasks and tags. Copies of OneDrive, iCloud and Google Drive (`Note 2.md`) cannot be told from a real note by name and stay ordinary notes (NET-015, #475).
