### Added

- The search has two new words, `orphan` (a note or drawing with no link in or out) and `no-backlinks` (nothing links to it; it may link out). A link to itself, a red link and a link to media outside the vault do not count. They work in the sidebar, the full search, the quick switcher and board lanes, combine with `AND`, `OR` and `NOT`, and have the rows **is an orphan** and **has no backlinks** in the filter form (#724).
