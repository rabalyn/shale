### Added

- Layout: Mail can be placed in the right sidebar, like Files, Search and Tags: drag its icon to a right menubar or to the right sidebar's content and answer **Move or copy?**, or right-click it for **Move to / Copy to the right sidebar**. The pane is a compact companion of the Mail tab: the accounts, the folders with their unread counts and the message list with the unread state. A click on a message opens it in the Mail tab (opening the tab when there is none). The pane keeps its own folder choice and scroll, separate from the tab, while the mail data and the sync are shared. Copy keeps the ribbon icon, which still opens the tab; old layouts load as they were. Feeds follows in a later step (#610).
- Layout: Calendar can be placed in the right sidebar the same way (not to be confused with the older Calendar panel). The pane is a compact companion of the Calendar tab: a small month with ‹ › and Today, today marked and a dot under the days with events, and the agenda of the next 14 days from the chosen day, all-day events first, each with its time and title. A click on a day moves the agenda there; a click on an event opens it in the Calendar tab (opening the tab when there is none) with its details. The pane keeps its own month and chosen day, separate from the tab, while the events and the sync are shared (#610).
