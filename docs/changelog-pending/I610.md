### Added

- Layout: Mail can be placed in the right sidebar, like Files, Search and Tags: drag its icon to a right menubar or to the right sidebar's content and answer **Move or copy?**, or right-click it for **Move to / Copy to the right sidebar**. The pane is a compact companion of the Mail tab: the accounts, the folders with their unread counts and the message list with the unread state. A click on a message opens it in the Mail tab (opening the tab when there is none). The pane keeps its own folder choice and scroll, separate from the tab, while the mail data and the sync are shared. Copy keeps the ribbon icon, which still opens the tab; old layouts load as they were. Calendar and Feeds follow in later steps (#610).
