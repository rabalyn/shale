### Added

- **Thesis writing in the LaTeX export**: citations (`[@key, p. 12]`, `@key`) become `biblatex` commands with biber and the list of references at the end or at `<!-- shale: bibliography -->`; figures and tables with a label (`![[plot.png|Results]]{#fig:results}`, `: The caption {#tbl:runs}`) become numbered floats with their caption (tables above and figures below by default, configurable with `table-caption:` and `figure-caption:` or the template set's `table_caption` and `figure_caption`); equations with a label get one number; references (`@fig:results`, `@sec:method`) are written as "Abbildung 3" or "Figure 3" in the language of the note; numbers count per chapter in a book and continuously in an article (`float-numbering:`). The export dialog has the boxes **List of figures**, **List of tables**, **Bibliography** and the choice of the **citation style**, and a template set's `settings.toml` can set `citation_style`. The packages `caption`, `cleveref`, `biblatex-apa` and `biblatex-ieee` join the managed TeX Live's list (#647).
