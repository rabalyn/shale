### Fixed

- **Selected rows, hovered buttons and every other text on a coloured background read in every theme.** In Catppuccin Mocha light the selected row of a dropdown was pink text on salmon. egui drew the text of a selected row in the accent, which nothing checked against the selection colour. Now the text of selected, hovered, pressed and open widgets is the accent or the text colour, whichever reads (WCAG 4.5), and every pair of colours the app draws (text on tinted backgrounds, links, tags, status colours, code colours, the highlight) gets the contrast it needs when the theme is made: a colour that is too faint is moved towards black or white, a tint that is too strong is made weaker. Your own theme files get the same. Solarized Light's comment colour and Shale's faint grey and comment colour are a little stronger (#691).
