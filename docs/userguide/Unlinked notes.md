![[unlinked.png]]

The **Unlinked notes** view (the icon of two circles with a broken line, after the keyboard in the ribbon) lists the notes and
drawings that no link connects, so you can link them, merge them or let them go. Back to [[Start here]].

- A switch shows one group at a time: **Orphans** (no link in and none out) or **No backlinks** (nothing links to the file; it may link out).
- The sort button orders the list by **date changed** (newest first, the default, or oldest first) or by **name**.
  *Date changed* is the day the body of the note was last changed, the `modified:` property of [[Editor]]; a note
  without it and a drawing use the time of the file.
- A click on a name opens the note or the drawing. With none, the view says so.
- A link to the note itself, a link to a note that does not exist, and a link to a picture, a PDF or another file do not
  count as links. A link from a drawing does ([[Drawings]]).
- The same two groups are the search words `orphan` and `no-backlinks` ([[Search syntax]]), which also work in the
  sidebar, the quick switcher and the lanes of a [[Boards]] board.

An Unlinked notes tab comes back after a restart with its switch and its sort, see [[Tabs]]. How the notes link: [[Notes and links]],
[[Graph and backlinks]].
