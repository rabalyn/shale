**Ctrl+E** switches between editing and the reading view of a note. Back to [[Start here]].

![[reading.png]]

The reading view shows the note rendered: headings, lists, links (blue and red as in the
editor), embedded pictures, drawings and PDFs, and task boxes you can tick (the change is saved
in the note). Coloured highlights and font colours (`<mark style="background: …">`, `<span style="color: …">`, see [[Editor]]) are shown with their tags hidden. Footnote references are small raised labels: a click on one scrolls to its definition, a click on the label of a definition scrolls back (see [[Editor]]). `<u>`, `<sub>` and `<sup>` give underlined, lowered and raised text, and `<br>` in a table cell starts a new line. Tables are drawn as grids, with a bold header row and aligned columns. Headings carry their numbers when numbering is on, and a link to a heading (`[[Note#Heading]]`) scrolls the reading view to it without leaving it (see [[Notes and links]]). If you only want to read, this is the quiet way to do it. The always-rendered
alternative while you edit is the Markdown preview in [[Editor]].
