The right sidebar shows, for the open note: **backlinks** (who links here), **unlinked
mentions** (the note's name in plain text), **outgoing links**, the **outline** (headings shown formatted and numbered, as in the editor; a click scrolls to the heading, in the editor or the reading view, see [[Notes and links]]) and a **local
graph**. The **vault graph** (ribbon) shows every note: red dots are missing notes (links
nobody wrote yet) and green ones are drawings. Links in drawings count too ([[Drawings]]).

Try it: this note is linked from [[Start here]], and it links to [[Tasks]], [[Drawings]] and
[[Window layout]]. Open the graph and find it.

How to arrange the panels: [[Window layout]].

A Graph tab comes back after a restart with its switches (missing notes, orphans), see [[Tabs]].
