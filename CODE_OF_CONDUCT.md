# Code of conduct

This applies to the GitHub issues of Shale (<https://github.com/rabalyn/shale>), pull requests and
any other space the project runs.

## What we ask

- Be kind and assume good faith. People write here in a second language, in a hurry or in pain
  over a lost note.
- Criticise the work, not the person. "This does not work because …" is welcome. Insults are not.
- Stay on the topic of the issue. Keep one problem to one issue.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, threats and insults.
- Discrimination, or attacks on people for who they are.
- Sexualised content and unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information about anyone (doxxing).
- Spam and advertising.

## About AI

Shale is built by AI coding agents, and the README says so. Disagreement with that, or with
software made that way, is welcome when it is civil. Campaigns against the people involved, such as
brigading, pile-ons or harassment of the owner or of contributors, are not.

## What the maintainer may do

The maintainer may edit or hide a comment, lock a thread, close an issue and block an account,
with or without a warning, when this code is broken. The decision is the maintainer's.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub's private reporting:
<https://github.com/rabalyn/shale/security/advisories/new>. Say that it is a conduct report, and
link the comments. You can also use "Report content" on GitHub, which tells GitHub as well.

Reports are kept confidential. The name of the person who reported is not given to the person
reported.
